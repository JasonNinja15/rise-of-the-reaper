# Rise of the Reaper

A top-down skeleton-slaying game for one to six players. Reap the bone legion with your scythe, necromance broken squads into your own army, and spend their souls in the Soul Forge.

**Play:** https://jasonninja15.github.io/rise-of-the-reaper/

## On a Chromebook

Two ways to keep the game without needing the site every time:

- **Install it as an app.** Open the game in Chrome, then click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (or **⋮ → Cast, save, and share → Install page as app**). It gets its own icon in the launcher and opens in its own window. Once it has loaded one time, solo play works with no internet.
- **Download the file.** Click **Download to play offline** on the title screen. It saves `rise-of-the-reaper.html` to your Downloads. Open it from the **Files** app (double-click, or right-click → Open with → Chrome) any time.

Solo play needs nothing else. Playing together still needs an internet connection. If your school has blocked the site, downloads, or app installs on a managed Chromebook, those rules apply. Ask your IT admin rather than working around them.

## Playing together

1. One player opens the game, presses **Play together**, picks **Co-op** or **PvP + PvE**, and presses **Create room**.
2. Share the five-character room code.
3. Everyone else presses **Play together**, types the code, and presses **Join**.
4. The host presses **Begin the Harvest**. Players who join later drop straight into the running game.

No account is needed. Players connect browser-to-browser over WebRTC, using the free public [PeerJS](https://peerjs.com) server to find each other. The room's creator hosts the dead, so if the host leaves, the room closes. Some strict school or office networks block these direct connections.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Move |
| Mouse | Aim the scythe |
| Left click / J | Sweeping reap (hold to keep swinging) |
| Space | Roll |
| Right click | Necromance the bones of a squad you have wiped out |
| Shift (hold) | Send your risen toward the cursor |
| E | Soul Forge |
| Q | Soulfire, once you have earned it |
| Mouse wheel, + / − | Zoom |
| M | Mute |
| Esc | Pause (in a room, the world keeps going) |

## On phones and tablets

Touch controls appear automatically. Drag anywhere on the left side to move. Drag the **Reap** stick at the bottom right to aim and swing, or tap it to strike the nearest foe. **Roll**, **Raise** (necromance), **Lead** (hold to send your risen ahead) and **Fire** (Soulfire) sit beside it, with zoom and pause buttons under the health bar. Landscape gives the widest view.
